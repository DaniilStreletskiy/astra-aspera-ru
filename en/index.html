Space strategy in development

A galaxy with a past

By the time a civilisation sends its first ship to another galaxy, generations have already shaped its course. There were discoveries worth crossing the stars for, neighbours who became allies, and borders drawn after a war. Astra & Aspera is a strategy game built around the idea of simulating an entire universe. You guide one civilisation through it. Trace a frontier or a trade route back through time and you should be able to see whose decisions put it there.

When can the expedition leave?

Two ships are due at the station at minute 20. One carries fuel. The other brings the crew, who need three minutes for their checks. The expedition can depart as soon as both are ready. Use the chart to see which arrival actually sets the departure time.

At galactic scale, a changed event can have thousands of dependants. The hard part is finding where its effect stops. Here, you can follow the links backwards from departure to see which event determined it.

What it takes to build this galaxy

Game

Astra & Aspera

What makes a planet habitable? A human colony needs food. A digital society needs computing capacity, power and somewhere to dump waste heat. The choice of inhabitants changes what it makes sense to build first.

Simulation engine

Actors & Aggregates

A shipment arrives late. Construction that needs it must move; a mission in another system can keep its date. This engine project asks how to find the affected events and how to check that a second run produces the same sequence.

Research practice

Agents & Artifacts

An AI agent proposes a design. Which assumptions went into it? What test could expose a mistake? This project traces a proposal through review and experiment so another reader can see why it was accepted or set aside.
